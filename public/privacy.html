Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2025

Overview

Pull (pull.global) is a location-based audio discovery platform. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it. We are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently.

Listener Data

Pull does not require listeners to create an account. You can discover and play audio nodes on the map without providing any personal information.

We do collect the following data in connection with listener activity:

  • Play events. When you play a node, Pull records that a play occurred on that node. This count is anonymous — we do not associate it with your identity or device.
  • Play timestamp. Pull records the time at which a listener plays a song in order to support engagement analytics and artist reporting.
  • Listen duration. Pull tracks the length of time a listener plays a given song or audio node. This data helps artists understand how their content is being received and is used to improve the platform experience.

Play counts, timestamps, and listen durations are aggregated and shared with artists as engagement metrics. This data is not sold or used for advertising purposes.

Voluntary Information

Pull may invite users — both listeners and artists — to voluntarily provide additional information to help us improve the platform and better serve our community. This includes:

  • Email address. You may choose to provide your email address to receive platform updates, new feature announcements, or communications from Pull. Providing an email address is optional and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Demographic information. Pull may optionally collect demographic information such as age range, location, and musical interests. This information is used exclusively to understand our user base and improve the platform. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

All voluntarily submitted information is collected only with your consent. You may request deletion of any voluntarily provided data at any time by contacting us at info@pull.global.

Artist Data

Artists who upload audio to Pull provide content metadata (title, description, location, scheduling) and an email address. This information is used to operate the platform, communicate about your content, and provide support. We do not sell or share artist data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

How We Use Data

  • To operate location-based audio discovery
  • To provide artists with engagement analytics (play counts, listen duration, play timing)
  • To communicate with artists about their content and account
  • To improve the platform through aggregated, anonymized usage patterns
  • To send platform updates to users who have opted in with their email address

Third Parties

Pull uses infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, database services) to run the platform. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver the service and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing under any circumstances.

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored using industry-standard security practices. Listen data and play counts are anonymized wherever possible. Artist account information is protected with access controls and encrypted in transit.

Children

Pull is not directed at users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Artists can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their email address and content metadata at any time. Users who have voluntarily provided demographic or email information may request deletion of that data as well.

To make a request, contact us at info@pull.global.

Policy Updates

Pull may update this policy from time to time. Artists will be notified of material changes by email. For all other users, the updated policy will be posted on this page. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
